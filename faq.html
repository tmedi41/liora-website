FAQ

Good to know before you book your Houston photo booth.

Answers to the questions we hear most from couples, planners, and brands.

Are you insured?

Yes. Liora Photo Co. carries full general liability insurance, and we're happy to provide a certificate of insurance (COI) directly to your venue ahead of your event.

What does pricing start at?

Our experiences start at $XXX. Every proposal is a single all-inclusive number covering travel, setup, teardown, and your full digital gallery. The number on your proposal is the number on your invoice.

What's the setup and teardown process?

Our attendant arrives 60–90 minutes before your service window to set up the booth, backdrop, and printing station. Teardown takes about 30 minutes after your window ends. Both are included and don't count against your booked hours.

How many prints do guests get?

Unlimited. Every guest can print as many strips or copies as they like throughout your service window. Everyone leaves with a keepsake.

What space and power do you need?

We ask for roughly 8 ft × 8 ft of level floor space and a standard power outlet within 15 ft. We're happy to do a walkthrough with your venue ahead of time.

Do you travel outside Houston?

Yes, we regularly travel throughout the greater Houston area and beyond. Let us know your venue location and we'll confirm any travel fee.

How do we book?

Send us your date through our contact form. Once we confirm availability, a signed agreement and deposit secure your date.

Still have questions?

We're happy to talk it through.

Send us a note and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
