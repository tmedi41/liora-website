Straight answers

Your questions,
answered

What if the camera or printer fails?+

A second camera body, printer, and laptop travel to every event. Swapping takes about four minutes.

How early do you arrive?+

90 minutes before your start time, with a test print done before guests arrive. We coordinate with your planner directly.

What do you need from my venue?+

An 8 × 8 ft footprint, one standard outlet, and cover if we're outdoors. We bring the table, cable runs, and lighting.

Are you insured?+

Fully. We carry $2M general liability and send a certificate naming your venue the same day you ask, which is what most venues require before load-in.

Do you charge for travel or idle time?+

Not inside the Houston metro. If your reception has a two-hour dinner break, we idle at no charge and come back on.
